HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from city name to full climate risk briefing.

01
Enter City
Type any city name worldwide. Nominatim geocodes it to precise lat/lng coordinates.
02
Fetch Data
Open-Meteo weather and air quality APIs return live readings in parallel.
03
Calculate Risk
Threshold algorithms score each hazard; a weighted formula produces the composite index.
04
View Report
Color-coded cards, gauge, risk bars, and map render instantly with alert banners.
